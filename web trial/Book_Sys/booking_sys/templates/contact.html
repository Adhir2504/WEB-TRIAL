Common Questions

How long does it take to get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days.

What if I need urgent assistance?

For urgent issues, please contact the facility manager directly or visit the administration office during business hours.

How do I cancel or reschedule a booking?

You can cancel or reschedule bookings from your profile page. Simply click on the booking you want to modify.

What are the facility operating hours?

General hours are 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Monday to Saturday). Check individual facility details for specific hours.
